Our Process

From Seed to Bottle — With Care

We follow a strict and hygienic process to ensure the highest quality in every product.

Home / Our Process

🌾

1. Seed Selection

Mustard seeds and groundnuts are hand-picked from trusted regional farmers for size, colour and oil content.

🧹

2. Cleaning

Every batch is cleaned to remove dust, husk and foreign matter before it goes anywhere near the press.

🛢️

3. Oil Extraction

Cold Kachi Ghani pressing at low temperature — no heat, no hexane — to preserve natural aroma and nutrients.

🧪

4. Filtration

Freshly pressed oil is filtered in stages to remove sediment while keeping its natural colour and richness.

🔬

5. Quality Testing

Each batch is checked for purity, aroma and consistency before it's cleared for packing.

📦

6. Packaging

Sealed and labelled in food-grade containers designed to protect freshness till it reaches your kitchen.

🚚

7. Distribution

Delivered through our growing network of dealers across India — Desi Swad, Har Ghar Tak.
